## 4. Arithmetic Interpretation Check

The scoper's handoff explicitly flagged this: r1 §7 estimate-realism stated "33.5h total" but literal arithmetic before the per-task r2 adds (Minor-2 +0.25h on M3.verify.2; §4.2 +0.25h on M3.verify.6) gives 33.0h. **Verdict: interpretation (a) is correct — the scoper's framing holds.**

Reasoning:

1. The r1 §7 critic estimate table headline of 33.5h was derived from the scoper's per-milestone calibration (M0 = 1.00h, M1 = 4.25h, M2 = 3.50h, M3 = 13.00h, M4 = 2.75h, M5 = 3.00h, M6 = 4.00h, M7 = 2.00h = 33.50h). That sum still holds at r2 because none of the milestone-level numbers changed.

The two +0.25h per-task adds in r2 (M3.verify.2 and M3.verify.6) raise the **task-level sum within M3** by +0.5h but were absorbed inside M3's stated 13.0h milestone total without bumping the milestone number. The plan's revision-history block at lines 42–48 documents this explicitly: "These +0.5h of cumulative per-task additions in M3 are absorbed within the critic's recommended 33.5h plus-or-minus 6h headline ... If the Minor / §4 additions push M3 above 13.5h in practice, the variance lands inside the confidence band."
3. The plus-or-minus 6h band (range 27.5h to 39.5h) easily covers a 0.5h drift in either direction. The drift is bounded, named, and documented in the right places.

**No reconciliation needed.** The headline stays at 33.5h plus-or-minus 6h.
